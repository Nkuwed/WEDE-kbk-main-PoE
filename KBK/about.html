Meet the artist

Meet Keora

Nail technician based in Kibler Park, Johannesburg South — building a loyal client base since her social media launch in 2023.

Her journey

Keora's move into nail artistry began with a love of art and a want to keep exploring her creative side. Becoming a qualified nail technician gave her a canvas for that — and a new challenge to grow into with every set she creates.

Business background & challenges

KreatedByKeora is a real, independently operated studio, formed in early 2023. Keora built her client base almost entirely through TikTok and Instagram, taking bookings by direct message and comment section. As the following grew, that system started to show its limits: no centralised price list, no formal booking process, and low visibility to anyone outside her existing followers — which is exactly what this website sets out to fix.

Studio philosophy

Every appointment is treated as a small collaboration — quality and creativity first, at a price that keeps affordable, high-craft nail art accessible to every client who sits in the chair.

Mission

Creative, high-quality, affordable nail artistry — accessible to every client, while growing into a recognised professional brand in Johannesburg South.

Vision

To grow from a home/mobile-based studio into a go-to nail destination, and one day partner with major brands for seasonal promotional events.

Who it's for

Women aged 18 to 45 in and around Kibler Park and greater Johannesburg, looking for Gel-X extensions and dramatic nail art.
